- BM25/CJK retrieval with optional embeddings and RRF hybrid fusion.

  Obsidian Headless sidecar, systemd examples, and private/cloud networking
  guidance.
- Unit, integration, retrieval-evaluation, syntax, dependency, and secret-scan
  release gates.
