# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data, and hostile or
disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

When reviewing work, address the change and its evidence rather than the
person. Assume good faith while still treating security and privacy claims
with appropriate rigor. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions
and restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile or another private channel they publish. Do not include sensitive
personal information in a public issue.
